What we do

Complete care for every smile.

General, cosmetic, and surgical dentistry for the whole family — with sedation options available for an easy, comfortable visit.

Preventive

Exams, Cleanings & Sealants

Routine checkups, professional cleanings, sealants, and fluoride to keep problems small or prevent them entirely.

Restorative

Fillings, Crowns & Bridges

Tooth-colored fillings, crowns, and bridges that restore strength and blend in naturally.

Cosmetic

Root Canals, TMJ & Gums

Root canal treatment, TMJ therapy, and periodontal care to relieve pain and protect your oral health.

Meet your dentists

Dr. Tatum & Dr. Cox.

The experienced team leading The Smile Spot of Auburn — delivering caring, technologically advanced dentistry and hygiene the whole family can trust.

Dr. Crawford Tatum
DMD · General, Cosmetic & Implant Dentistry

Dr. Crawford A. Tatum Jr. brings extensive experience in general, cosmetic, and implant dentistry. He is affiliated with leading organizations including the American Dental Association and the American Academy of Implant Dentistry.

His focus is restoring healthy, confident smiles with precision, modern technology, and a genuinely caring touch.

Dr. Christina Cox
DMD · Family & Cosmetic Dentistry

Dr. Christina Cox provides comprehensive family and cosmetic dentistry with a gentle, patient-first approach that puts people of all ages at ease.

She believes great dentistry starts with listening — and takes the time to make sure every patient understands and feels confident in their care.
